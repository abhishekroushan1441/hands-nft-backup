Introduction

Welcome to HandsNFT AI

HandsNFT AI is a game where strategy, verifiable on-chain mechanics, and blockchain innovation merge in a competitive and decentralized gaming ecosystem. Your journey begins by minting Hand Cards, progressing through levels, and competing for rewards as you aim to achieve the elite Diamondhand status.

HandsNFT AI is designed to be fully decentralized, ensuring fairness and transparency through trustless, on-chain mechanics. Unlike traditional blockchain games, this game does not use a tokenized economy—meaning players are not required to stake tokens or make additional investments to participate. Instead, they pay a predefined minting fee to enter the game and engage in strategic gameplay.

In HandsNFT AI, Hand Cards function as unique in-game assets, allowing players to progress through levels and access new gameplay mechanics. At the end of each season, players who reach Level 4 (Diamondhand) may qualify to receive an exclusive Ethereum-based NFT, unlocking additional participation benefits in future seasons.

The game introduces a claim-based reward system, eliminating the need for staking and unstaking mechanisms. Each Hand Card has a designated timer that must complete before players can claim available rewards. After claiming, the Level Up button activates, allowing players to attempt an upgrade through on-chain verifiable randomness. Success grants access to higher-level pools, while failure resets the timer for another attempt.

This structure ensures that HandsNFT AI operates independently of market volatility, as rewards are derived from a predetermined, non-tokenized system rather than external token price fluctuations. Players can continuously engage, progress, and compete within a stable and transparent ecosystem.

HandsNFT AI is more than just a game—it's a test of patience, risk assessment, and strategic decision-making. Whether your goal is to optimize gameplay or reach the elite Diamondhand status, HandsNFT AI offers a dynamic, decentralized gaming experience where blockchain technology enhances competition and engagement.

Are you ready to compete, level up, and test your strategy? The journey starts now!

How It Works

A step-by-step guide to playing HandsNFT AI and mastering the game mechanics

How It Works

The HandsNFT AI Game combines strategic blockchain gameplay with unique mechanics. Here's a step-by-step guide:

Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

  • Visit https://handsnft.fun/
  • Connect MetaMask or supported wallet

Step 2: Mint Your Hand Card

  • Navigate to the "Mint" section.
  • Enter the number of Hand Cards you want to mint and proceed. Remember! The more and higher-level Hand Cards you own, the greater your chances of earning higher rewards!

Step 3: Claim Your Rewards

  • Once you mint your Hand Cards, the timer starts counting down.
  • Each level has a predefined timer duration, and once it expires, the Claim button will be activated under your Hand Card.
  • Click the Claim button to compete for available rewards based on the pool mechanics.

Anti-Bot Rewards System

To preserve fairness in HandsNFT AI, we've introduced a new protection layer:

  1. Rewards Pool: Every time you claim, your rewards are now held in a personal pool instead of being instantly sent to your wallet.

  2. Withdrawal Timer: You can only withdraw your rewards after a short cooldown — the exact time is shown in the app.

  3. AI Detection in Action: During this period, our AI-based detection tool actively scans all wallets in real time, identifying suspicious behavior patterns. Any wallet flagged for automation, botting, or irregular actions will be blocked from withdrawing.

Step 4: Level Up Your Hand Card

  • After successfully claiming rewards from your Hand Card, the Level Up button will be activated.
  • To access higher-level pools, you can attempt to upgrade your Hand Card through a verifiable on-chain process.
  • Leveling up is influenced by a transparent, on-chain calculation system that introduces an element of variability while maintaining fairness and decentralization. The probability of a successful upgrade ranges from 80% to 30%, with slight adjustments in each season's game mechanics.

If an upgrade attempt is unsuccessful, you can wait for the Claim Timer to expire, claim available rewards again, and then make another attempt at leveling up. While upgrading involves a measurable probability, the overall game progression remains centered around player strategy and timely actions to maximize participation.

Step 5: Migrate

  • When any of your Hand Cards reach Level 4, also known as the DiamondHand level, you have the option to migrate it.
  • A migrated Hand Card can no longer claim rewards, signifying its completion in the current season.
  • At the end of the season, each owner of a migrated DiamondHand will be eligible to receive a unique NFT from HandsNFT AI's exclusive collection on the Ethereum network. These NFTs serve as a recognition of participation and will be distributed transparently through an on-chain process.
  • NFTs will be sent directly to eligible users' wallets, ensuring fair and decentralized distribution. Ownership of these NFTs grants automatic WL (whitelist) access for the next seasons and may provide additional benefits in future special seasons, as announced by the HandsNFT AI team.

Levels and Pools

Understanding the four levels of Hand Cards and their reward pools

Levels and Pools

In the HandsNFT AI Game, players progress through four unique Hand Cards levels, each offering distinct opportunities for rewards and growth. Here's what you need to know about the levels:

Paperhand Card (Level 1)

  • Description: The entry-level for all players, representing newcomers or those just starting their journey.
  • Rewards: No claimable rewards at this level—this phase is focused on experience-building.
  • Claim Timer: Shortest timer duration.
  • Goal: Gain experience and attempt to level up.

Sandhand Card (Level 2)

  • Description: A step above Paperhand, where players gain access to competitive in-game rewards.
  • Rewards: Small in-game rewards plus experience points (EXP).
  • Claim Timer: Slightly longer than Level 1.
  • Distribution: A large number of players are eligible for rewards, but at lower amounts compared to higher levels.

Steelhand Card (Level 3)

  • Description: A mark of true commitment—players here have successfully leveled up and gained experience.
  • Rewards: Mid-tier rewards and additional EXP.
  • Claim Timer: Longer than Level 2.
  • Distribution: Fewer players receive rewards compared to Level 2, creating increased competition.

Diamondhand Card (Level 4)

  • Description: The highest-level milestone—only the most strategic and persistent players reach this elite tier.
  • Rewards: The most exclusive in-game rewards, along with the ability to migrate Hand Cards for a recognition-based Ethereum NFT.
  • Claim Timer: Longest duration.
  • Distribution: The majority of high-level rewards are allocated to this pool, making it the most competitive.

Climbing the Ranks

  • Higher levels provide access to a larger share of rewards. Diamondhand receives the most competitive opportunities, followed by Steelhand, then Sandhand.
  • Paperhand holders do not receive in-game claimable rewards but can gain experience points to progress.
  • As minting continues, rewards flow into the pools, maintaining a dynamic and skill-driven competition system.

Manage risk, time your claims strategically, and aim for Diamondhand status for the most challenging and rewarding gameplay experience.

Mechanics Changed/Improved in HandsNFT AI v2

Hand Card

Hand Cards are essential in-game assets required for participating in HandsNFT AI.

In the previous season, these assets were NFTs, but with v2, they have been transformed into non-transferable Hand Cards.

Why This Change?

  • Non-Transferability for Fair Competition: Tradable NFTs could allow external purchases that might create an unfair advantage in reward pools. To maintain fairness and balance, Hand Cards are now non-transferable and cannot be sold.
  • Streamlined User Experience: Eliminating staking and unstaking processes reduces gas fees and simplifies gameplay, making the experience more accessible and cost-effective.

No More Stake and Unstake

With Hand Cards, the staking and unstaking mechanism has been removed. Players can now directly:

  • Claim their available rewards.
  • Attempt to level up.
  • Upon reaching DiamondHand status, opt to migrate their Hand Card for a recognition-based NFT.

Migrate

Each season, HandsNFT AI will allow a limited number of DiamondHand Cards to be migrated. Through migration, players can burn a DiamondHand Card to receive an exclusive NFT from the HandsNFT AI NFT collection on the Ethereum blockchain. These NFTs will be distributed transparently through an on-chain process after the season ends.

Important Note: A migrated Hand Card can no longer claim rewards.

Pools and Prize Distribution

Rewards are automatically allocated to reward pools based on the number of minted Hand Cards. There are four pools:

  • Paperhand Pool
  • Sandhand Pool
  • Steelhand Pool
  • Diamondhand Pool

Each pool corresponds to a Hand Card level, with higher-level pools offering access to increased reward opportunities.

Key Considerations for Pools:

  • Claiming rewards follows a First Come, First Served (FCFS) system. Players must track their Claim Timers to maximize their participation in the pools.
  • Strategic Leveling Up Matters: Prioritizing higher-level pools can increase reward opportunities, but if higher-tier pools are depleted, lower-tier pools may offer an advantage.

Paperhand Pool:

  • This pool does not provide claimable rewards.
  • Players in this pool can gain experience points (EXP) to progress to higher levels.

HAI Dashboard

Players can now monitor real-time in-game activities, including the latest mints, claims, level-ups, and migrations. With the HAI Dashboard, players can develop strategic decisions based on observing in-game trends and competition movements.
